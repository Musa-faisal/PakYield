# PakYield Empirical Sample Scope

## Primary Research Window

The required v1 empirical analysis uses a primary PKRV sample beginning on:

**4 January 2022**

and continuing through the latest validated observation available during the
project's 2026 data-acquisition period.

## Rationale

MUFAP's current machine-readable pricing archive exposes a consistent daily
PKRV series from January 2022 onward.

The current MUFAP archive also exposes twelve monthly PKRV workbooks for 2020,
while the corresponding 2021 PKRV files are not present in the current API.

Legacy MUFAP listings indicate that 2021 PKRV observations existed, but the
former static file locations are no longer reliably available through the
current acquisition path.

PakYield therefore does not attempt to manufacture continuity across the
archive transition.

For the required v1 research project, the primary PKRV sample is restricted to
the reproducible daily series available through the current MUFAP system.

## Required v1 Samples

### PKRV

Primary sample:

**2022-01-04 through latest validated 2026 observation**

The 2020 monthly workbooks are not part of the required core analysis.

The 2021 period is excluded from required v1 because of archival discontinuity.

### PKISRV Comparative Analysis

Primary common-sample start:

**2025-02-03**

The comparison continues through the latest date for which validated PKRV and
PKISRV observations are both available.

No long-end PKISRV observations will be synthetically created.

### Monetary Policy Event Study

The required event-study period is:

**2022 through 2026**

MPC event windows remain based on available market observations rather than
calendar-day interpolation.

The event study remains descriptive unless a later identification strategy
supports causal interpretation.

### CPI

The primary analytical macroeconomic period begins in:

**January 2022**

Earlier CPI observations may be acquired when technically necessary to
validate year-over-year inflation calculations that require twelve-month lagged
index values.

Such supporting observations do not extend the primary empirical sample.

## Historical Material

### 2020

The twelve monthly PKRV workbooks exposed by the current MUFAP API may be
retained as optional historical or source-validation material.

They are not required for the primary v1 empirical analysis.

### 2021

PKRV data for 2021 are excluded from the required v1 research sample because
the current MUFAP machine-readable archive does not expose the corresponding
PKRV artifacts.

This exclusion must not be interpreted as evidence that PKRV observations did
not exist in 2021.

## Research Integrity

PakYield will not:

- fabricate missing 2021 observations;
- interpolate across the archival gap to create a synthetic history;
- describe 2020 monthly files as equivalent to the later daily archive without
  validation;
- imply that unavailable historical files never existed;
- extend conclusions beyond the validated analytical sample.

## Portfolio Interpretation

The chosen sample prioritizes:

- reproducibility;
- consistent source acquisition;
- transparent provenance;
- sufficient daily observations for yield-curve analysis;
- monetary-policy event analysis;
- term-spread analysis;
- Nelson-Siegel estimation;
- fixed-income risk analytics.

The archival discontinuity is treated as a research-design constraint rather
than silently repaired.
