# PakYield Lab — Phase 8 Research Publication Plan

## Status

ACTIVE

## Purpose

Phase 8 converts PakYield Lab's frozen analytical and research evidence into
professional research and portfolio deliverables without changing the
underlying methodology or empirical results.

The publication layer is downstream of the frozen research pipeline.

It may explain, organize, visualize, and communicate evidence.

It may not manufacture new empirical findings merely for narrative
completeness.

## Primary Phase 8 deliverables

### Phase 8A — Publication evidence packet and research architecture

Deliver:

- deterministic publication source packet generated from the frozen Phase 6D
  research evidence map;
- publication boundary and evidence-traceability rules;
- academic paper structure;
- two-page research-brief structure.

### Phase 8B — Academic research paper

Produce an academic-style paper containing:

1. title;
2. abstract;
3. research motivation;
4. institutional and market context;
5. research questions;
6. data and sample;
7. methodology;
8. descriptive evidence;
9. MPC announcement-window results;
10. monetary-policy regime results;
11. PKRV/PKISRV comparative results;
12. inferential findings where the frozen evidence supports them;
13. limitations;
14. conclusion;
15. reproducibility note;
16. references and source notes.

Each substantive result must be traceable to frozen project evidence.

### Phase 8C — Two-page research brief

Produce a concise decision-oriented brief containing:

- research question;
- data used;
- major findings;
- charts or tables only where useful;
- interpretation;
- limitations;
- implications for market/research audiences;
- project/repository reference.

The brief must not upgrade descriptive findings into causal findings.

### Phase 8D — Portfolio and launch package

Prepare:

- GitHub project presentation;
- LinkedIn launch copy;
- CV project bullets;
- interview-ready project explanation;
- concise technical architecture description;
- concise research-methodology description.

## Evidence hierarchy

Publication claims should use this priority:

1. frozen Phase 6 research evidence;
2. frozen Phase 5 analytical inputs;
3. validated normalized/production data;
4. documented methodology and decisions;
5. presentation-layer outputs only as displays of the above.

The Streamlit dashboard is not an independent source of empirical truth.

## Non-negotiable interpretation boundaries

Publication material must not:

- call an MPC event-window move a policy surprise without expectations data;
- infer causality from observational regime comparisons;
- treat missing values as zero;
- introduce hidden interpolation;
- reinterpret numerical PKISRV-PKRV differences as structural, Islamic,
  Sukuk, Shariah, liquidity, credit, or tax premia without separate evidence;
- state inferential significance where the frozen inferential layer does not
  support it.

## Remaining v1.0 deliverables outside the publication-writing task

The project specification also lists items including:

- Jupyter research notebooks;
- fixed-income risk engine;
- Nelson-Siegel implementation;
- final public portfolio/launch materials.

Phase 8 does not silently mark these complete.

They must either be implemented, explicitly deferred, or formally removed
from the v1.0 completion definition through a documented decision.

## Completion standard

Phase 8 publication outputs are complete only when:

- every empirical claim is evidence-traceable;
- descriptive and inferential evidence remain distinguishable;
- limitations accompany consequential interpretations;
- publication files contain no unsupported placeholders;
- the repository remains reproducible and technically healthy.
